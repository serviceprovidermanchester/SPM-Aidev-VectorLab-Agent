# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

**SPManchester Private Limited** and the maintainers of **VectorLab** are committed to providing a respectful, welcoming, inclusive, and collaborative environment for all contributors, community members, and users.

We welcome participants of all experience levels, backgrounds, and identities to engage constructively in our open-source repositories and discussions.

---

## Expected Standards

Community members and contributors are expected to:
- **Demonstrate Professional Respect:** Treat every contributor with courtesy and professional consideration, regardless of background, viewpoint, or skill level.
- **Provide Constructive Technical Critique:** Focus discussions on algorithmic accuracy, architectural merit, performance, and mathematical precision rather than personalities.
- **Foster Intellectual Honesty:** Be transparent about trade-offs, representation limitations, and mathematical edge cases in search and clustering algorithms.
- **Welcome Newcomers:** Offer patient guidance to contributors submitting their first pull requests or bug reports.
- **Respect Privacy:** Keep security vulnerability disclosures, private correspondence, and sensitive communications confidential as appropriate.

---

## Unacceptable Conduct

The following behaviors will not be tolerated in any community space or repository channel:
- Harassment, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language, jokes, or behavior targeting personal characteristics.
- Publishing private communications without explicit permission.
- Trolling, bad-faith disruption of discussions, or spamming.
- Inappropriate solicitation or unauthorized promotional activity.

---

## Enforcement & Reporting

The leadership and maintainers of **SPManchester Private Limited** oversee enforcement of this Code of Conduct.

If you encounter or observe unacceptable conduct:
1. Contact the governance team directly at **[info@spmanchester.com](mailto:info@spmanchester.com)** with the subject line `[Code of Conduct Incident]`.
2. Include links, screenshots, or detailed context regarding the incident.
3. All complaints will be reviewed promptly and confidentially within 48 hours.
4. Corrective actions may range from a private warning to temporary suspension or permanent bans from the project repository and organization spaces.
